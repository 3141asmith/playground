    A line becomes a surface.

    Every point on the outline sweeps a circle around the centre line. Points further from the line trace larger circles. An edge touching the line can close a solid; an outline away from it can leave a hole through the middle.

    The grid and measurements use millimetres as model units, rather than physical screen measurements. Straight edges make conical, cylindrical or flat surfaces. Draw one simple outline without crossing edges; cut faces are included for a partial revolution. Dimensions and measurements update live.
