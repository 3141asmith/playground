    REPEAT AROUND A POINT

    Find your symmetry.

    4 copies, one every 90?.

    Keep the full result to add details without repeating them. Turning symmetry off also keeps it. Undo restores the rotational setup.

    Edit the numbered outline; the pale copies follow. Drag the purple centre marker on a closed outline to reposition it. Original vertices and the centre snap to the grid; rotated copies keep their exact angles.

    Overlapping copies join into one shape. Separate copies remain separate parts in the STL. Some outlines have additional symmetry of their own.

    The complete shape is now editable without symmetry. Select an outline to change its vertices or curves. Reopen it to add vertices, or draw a new shape or cut-out. Undo restores rotational editing; Clear or a preset starts a fresh shape.

    A line becomes a surface.

    Every point on the outline sweeps a circle around the centre line. Points further from the line trace larger circles. An edge touching the line can close a solid; an outline away from it can leave a hole through the middle.

    The grid and measurements use millimetres as model units, rather than physical screen measurements. Straight edges make conical, cylindrical or flat surfaces. Cubic Bézier curves add rounded profiles using two control handles. Curved measurements and surfaces are approximated with short segments; STL exports use a finer approximation. Draw one simple outline without crossing edges; cut faces are included for a partial revolution. Extrusion adds a uniform thickness with closed front, back and side faces. Dimensions and measurements update live.
