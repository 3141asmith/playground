Where the moments come from

LoadWeight / NPosition / mPerpendicular distance / mMoment / N mDirection

Moment = force × perpendicular distance. Weights act vertically downwards, so the lever arm is the horizontal distance from the pivot to the force’s line of action. Equal opposing moments balance.

g = 9.81 m/s² · masses are attached to the beam · ideal fixed pivot with light rotational damping · no collisions. Release lets the beam rotate; Hold level returns it to the horizontal for a fresh comparison. Positions are measured from the left end.
