What’s included in the count?

Based on the supplied Mayfield 2026–2027 academic term dates (updated 18 October 2024). Normal school days are assumed to be Monday to Friday during term. Saturday 26 June, compulsory Mayfield Day / Prize Giving, is included.

Monday 3 May is excluded for the exeat closure. Staff INSET, induction for new staff, staff retreat and boarder arrival days are excluded. Pupil induction on 7 September is optional above. Term-ending and half-term-ending days count as whole school days, including early finishes.

This uses the general pupil timetable. Individual exam leave for Years 11 and 13 and the unconfirmed exam contingency date are not modelled. Regular Saturday lessons are not specified in the document and are not assumed.
