Drag R to change A and B equally. Their difference stays fixed, and their sum follows your cursor.

Two ways to explore

Move A or B to change its length and direction while keeping the other fixed. Move R to work backwards from the combined vector.

Right and up are positive. Directions are measured anticlockwise from +x. A zero vector has no defined direction.

Values use arbitrary units. Choose a larger grid for longer vectors. Focus an endpoint and use arrow keys; hold Shift for larger steps.

Components add independently. Rₓ = Aₓ + Bₓ and Rᵧ = Aᵧ + Bᵧ. The resultant’s magnitude is √(Rₓ² + Rᵧ²).

Dragging R shares its change equally: A′ = A + ΔR/2 and B′ = B + ΔR/2. This is one of many pairs of vectors with the same resultant. All endpoints stay inside the selected grid.
