Pattern versus particle

A transverse wave transfers energy along the medium, while the particles move perpendicular to that direction.

Pause to inspect a snapshot, step through the motion, or reverse the propagation. Reversing keeps the wave’s current shape continuous.

Wave model: y(x, t) = A sin(2πx/λ − φ), with dφ/dt = ±2πf. The sign controls the direction of propagation. A particle at fixed x oscillates with period T = 1/f.

This is an ideal sinusoidal transverse wave on an unbounded medium: no damping or reflections. Distances are in metres, time in seconds. Playback speed changes how quickly simulated time advances. Changing A or λ changes the shape immediately.
