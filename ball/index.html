Read the motion. These graphs follow vertical motion: distance is the ball’s centre height above the floor, and upward velocity and acceleration are positive. Horizontal movement stays visible in the playground.

During free fall, acceleration is −g. Bounces and your hand change the velocity, producing acceleration spikes. Impacts are averaged over each plotted interval (1/60 s); graph scales adjust automatically. Pausing freezes time and the graphs.

Ideal gravity and wall collisions · no air resistance · space is 10 × 6 metres
