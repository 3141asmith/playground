How to build a paper

  1. Add source PDFs. The tool reads their text and lists detected questions for each paper.
  2. Choose a question from the dropdown, preview it and add it to your set. You can also select regions manually. Include any shared instructions, diagrams and answer spaces needed.
  3. For a continuation, select its region on the next page and use “Add to selected question”.
  4. Optionally choose a source cover page, edit its fields and check each question’s marks. The cover total updates from your set.
  5. Upload mark scheme PDFs separately, pair each source paper with its scheme, and review the matched answers. Build the combined mark scheme to follow the same question order and numbering.
  6. Reorder the questions, choose the layout and build your combined PDF. Check the preview before downloading or printing.

Numbered headings are detected from embedded PDF text; formats vary, so check the estimated boundaries. Scanned PDFs need manual region selection. Renumbering replaces detected main numbers and decimal subpart prefixes on white backgrounds. Continuation notices are updated for the compiled layout. Other references within question prose are not rewritten. Notices inside scanned images cannot be read automatically; check the combined PDF. Password-protected PDFs are not supported. Only selected page regions are included; interactive form fields and annotations are not retained.
