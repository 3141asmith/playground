Why does the speed level off? As an object speeds up, drag increases. Terminal velocity is reached when drag balances its weight in air (or effective weight in liquid), so its acceleration becomes zero.

Drag increases with fluid density, frontal area and the square of speed. A parachute greatly increases area. In liquid, buoyancy reduces the object's effective weight; the object's volume controls its displaced-fluid buoyancy.
